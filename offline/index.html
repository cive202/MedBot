What are the classic warning signs that someone needs emergency care?Routine
Okay, I understand. You're asking about warning signs that would require emergency care. Some classic warning signs that would warrant emergency care include:
  • Difficulty breathing: This could indicate a serious respiratory problem.
  • Chest pain or pressure: This could be a sign of a heart attack or other serious cardiac issue.
  • New confusion: This could be a sign of a stroke or other neurological problem.
  • Inability to wake: This could be a sign of a serious medical condition.
  • Pale, grey, or blue lips or face: This could indicate a lack of oxygen.
  • Severe asthma attack: If you can't speak in full sentences, your lips are turning blue, or your reliever inhaler isn't working, call emergency services.
  • Very high blood glucose with confusion: This could be a sign of diabetic ketoacidosis (DKA) or hyperglycemic hyperosmolar state (HHS).
  • Peritonitis: This is a serious infection of the abdominal cavity, often caused by a ruptured appendix.
  • Severe dehydration: This can be dangerous, especially for young children and the elderly.
